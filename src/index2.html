About Us

Industry-Focused PCB Design Training

DhanaSree PCB Solutions provides industry-oriented PCB design training with hands-on practical exposure using industry-standard tools. We prepare students and professionals for real-world electronics and embedded design applications.

Who We Train

Students, working professionals, engineers, and hobbyists looking for job-ready PCB design skills.

What You Get

Live sessions, practical lab exercises, project mentorship, and deliverables ready for fabrication.

Tools We Use

Industry Standard EDA & PCB Layout Tools

Hands-on training on the same tools used by top electronics companies worldwide.

OrCAD

Learn OrCAD workflows for schematic capture, PCB layout, and manufacturing outputs including Gerber and NC drill file generation.

Allegro

Learn Allegro workflows for advanced schematic capture, high-speed PCB layout, constraint management, and manufacturing outputs.

Altium Designer

Learn Altium Designer workflows for unified schematic capture, PCB layout, 3D visualization, and complete fabrication outputs.
